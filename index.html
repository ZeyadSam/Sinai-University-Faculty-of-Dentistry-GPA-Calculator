I/P/NC/W
Official transcript notationsNot zero-point GPA grades

I (Incomplete), P (Pass), NC (No Credit) and W (Withdrawal) are transcript notations, not zero-point letter grades. P earns degree credit but does not affect GPA; I, NC and W are excluded from GPA and earned-credit totals. F contributes zero points and remains in GPA hours, but is not an earned credit. Weighted percentage is displayed only when actual marks are entered for every GPA-counted course; it is never inferred from GPA.
